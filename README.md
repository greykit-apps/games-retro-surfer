# Retro Surfer '96

**Ride the information superhighway, with Clippy on your tail.** A 32×32 pixel-art SkiFree/Surf-style easter egg themed around 90s web surfing and security: a puma on a board, carving down a slope of dead software.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/retrosurfer96](https://greykit.com/games/retrosurfer96/)

## How it plays

Distance is scored in KB downloaded, and speed reads out in baud. Size says what is coming: **small hazards** (Netscape, AOL CDs, Winamp, blue screens, BonziBuddy and more) cost a life; **large sludge** (popup storms, UNDER CONSTRUCTION zones, framesets) is harmless but drops you to a crawl; **glowing green pickups** give a shield, a speed boost, an extra life, or a chain of hyperlinks to ride. Every few thousand KB the world crosses into a new era, from 1994 dial-up to 2001 spyware, with its own colours and junk.

**Clippy** chases from behind at a fixed speed. You always travel along the board, so carving to dodge costs descent and hands it ground; pointing straight and tucking pulls away. Near misses pay a bonus that chains up to 3×, and a jump meter fills with clean riding.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → | Lean the board; tap to nudge, hold to carve |
| ↓ | Tuck, for speed |
| ↑ | Brake |
| Space | Jump; hold to stay up |
| Enter | Start, restart |

On a touchscreen, one finger is a virtual stick and a second finger jumps. The game pauses when it loses focus.

## Saves

The high score is kept in the browser's `localStorage` under `retroSurferHighScore`.
